Each input amplitude splits through H into both outputs. The solid lines show the "straight" paths (α to α', β to β'); the dashed lines show the crossing paths (α to β', β to α'). The coefficients are fixed: ×1/√2 everywhere except β to β', which gets ×−1/√2 (the minus sign). At each output, two contributions arrive and add. The values near the output dots show the pre-interference contributions; the output amplitude bars show their sum.
