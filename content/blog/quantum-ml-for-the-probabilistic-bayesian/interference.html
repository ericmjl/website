Measure now → probability = |amplitude|² (absolute 0–100% scale)
Every outcome sits at 12.5%, no matter the phases. A sign vanishes when squared, so phases are invisible here.
After the Hadamard gate → probability (absolute 0–100% scale; click a bar to inspect how it is built)

The Hadamard gate builds each output by adding up all 8 inputs with signs, its kernel gives each input a + or − (gray), that combines with your phase into a vote (blue + / red −), and the votes sum to the output's amplitude. Click a 0% output and you will see the votes balanced, half + and half −, canceling to zero. That visible cancellation is interference.
